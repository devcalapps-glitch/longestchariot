Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Developer: Longest Chariot LLC (Gilbert, Arizona, USA) • contact@longestchariot.com

Longest Chariot LLC ("we", "our", or "us") built the Lipi – Scan & Translate mobile and web application (Android package ID: com.longestchariot.lipi) as a Devanagari and English document scanner and translator. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on your device, what is sent to third-party services (translation providers, Google ML Kit, and Google AdMob advertising), and the choices you have.

100% On-Device OCR Photos Never Uploaded Camera images and PDFs are read locally on your phone via Google ML Kit Text Recognition v2.
Local Storage Only On-Device Scan History Saved scans stay in your device's local IndexedDB database and can be cleared anytime.
Ad-Supported Ads by Google AdMob No user accounts. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your Advertising ID. You can opt out of personalized ads anytime.

1. Camera, Photos, and Document Files (Processed On-Device Only)

Lipi requests access to your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) and selected media/document files solely when you tap "Take photo" or "Upload file" to scan a document.

  • All optical character recognition (OCR) for Devanagari (Nepali) and Latin (English) scripts runs locally on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition v2 (or in-browser WebAssembly OCR when used in a web browser).
  • Your raw photos, camera frames, and PDF files are never uploaded to Longest Chariot LLC servers or shared with third parties.

2. Extracted Text & Translation Requests

After text is extracted on your device, Lipi sends only the plain extracted text string over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) to a third-party online translation service (Google Translate or the MyMemory translation fallback) to return your Nepali or English translation in real time. This text is shared with that translation provider and is processed under its own privacy policy.

  • Longest Chariot LLC does not log, store, or inspect the text you translate on any company server.
  • You can edit the extracted text on your screen prior to re-translating, and we recommend exercising caution before translating confidential personal, medical, or financial records.

3. Google Play Data Safety & ML Kit Android SDK Disclosure

Longest Chariot LLC does not operate its own analytics servers. However, the bundled Google ML Kit Android SDK (com.google.mlkit:text-recognition-devanagari) automatically collects limited diagnostic and device telemetry, encrypted in transit via HTTPS, to maintain ML model reliability:

Data Category (Google Play Data Safety) Specific Data Handled Handling & Purpose
Photos & Videos / Files Camera captures, gallery images, and PDF pages selected by the user Processed strictly on-device. Not collected or transmitted off-device.
App Info & Performance (Diagnostics & Crash Logs) OCR processing latency, image resolution configuration, feature version, event types, and error codes Collected automatically by Google ML Kit SDK over HTTPS for App Functionality and Analytics. Not shared with third parties.
Device or Other IDs Per-installation identifiers (not uniquely tied to your identity), device model, and OS build Collected automatically by Google ML Kit SDK over HTTPS for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. Not shared with third parties.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lipi is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following information from your device and shares it with Google and its advertising partners:

  • Device or other IDs: the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app-set ID.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • App interactions: ad impressions, ad taps, and general app usage events.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, performance data, device model, and OS version.
  • Purposes: showing ads, ad measurement and analytics, fraud prevention and security, and — only where you have consented or where permitted by law — personalized advertising.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland): where required, Lipi shows Google's consent message before personalized ads are served. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options entry in Lipi's Settings.
  • Opting out: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your Advertising ID, which stops personalized ads. You can also manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • How Google uses this data: see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Longest Chariot LLC does not sell your personal information and does not receive your Advertising ID linked to your identity.

5. Local Storage, Data Retention, and Deletion Controls

When "Auto-save to History" is enabled or when you tap "Save to History", Lipi stores the document thumbnail, extracted text, and translated text locally on your device inside your browser/WebView IndexedDB database (lipi_db) and stores your UI preferences (theme, language, default translation direction) in localStorage.

  • Delete Single Scan: Open the History tab and tap the "×" button next to any saved item to delete it immediately from your device.
  • Delete All History: Open the Settings tab, tap "Delete" next to Clear History, and confirm in the slide-up sheet to wipe all IndexedDB records permanently.
  • Uninstalling the Lipi Android app also removes all local IndexedDB and WebView storage from your device.

6. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Lipi is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), does not require user registration, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Lipi are not intended for children. If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version with an updated effective date on this page and inside the app.
